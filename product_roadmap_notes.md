
# 🛡️ TrueHandover Product Roadmap: "Sticky" Utility Features
> **Philosophy:** High Retention ("Lock-In"), Zero AI, Zero Legal Liability.
> **Goal:** Become the "Operating System" for the freelancer, not just a delivery tool.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Workflow & Administration (The Admin Assistant)
*Tools that replace spreadsheets and scattered notes.*

### 1. The "Scope Creep" Pause Button
*   **Function:** A manual toggle in the dashboard. When active, the client sees a "Project Paused: Revision Limit Reached" overlay on the vault.
*   **Lock-In:** Gives the freelancer leverage without having to write an awkward email.
*   **Liability:** None. It is a status setting.

### 2. Time-Tracking Stopwatch
*   **Function:** Simple Start/Stop timer in the nav bar. Logs hours to specific projects.
*   **Lock-In:** Data gravity. Moving platforms means losing historical efficiency data.

### 3. Expense & Net-Profit Calculator
*   **Function:** Manual entry of project costs (stock photos, software subs). Calculates `Revenue - Expenses = Real Profit`.
*   **Lock-In:** Financial history is painful to export.

### 4. Reusable "Brand Kits"
*   **Function:** Store a client’s Hex codes and Logo once. Auto-apply them to future Vault themes.
*   **Lock-In:** Reduces setup time for repeat clients to near zero.

### 5. Interactive Checklists (SOPs)
*   **Function:** Freelancers save "Standard Operating Procedures" (e.g., "Web Launch Checklist"). These load as checkboxes for every new project.
*   **Lock-In:** Users build their business processes *into* the software.

## 🤝 Phase 2: Client Management (CRM Lite)
*Tools that manage the relationship, not just the file.*

### 6. The "Forever" Client Portal
*   **Function:** A persistent URL (`portal.truehandover.com/client-name`) showing *all* past and present projects for that specific client.
*   **Lock-In:** The client gets habituated to this hub. Moving platforms disrupts the client.

### 7. Automated "Nudge" Sequences
*   **Function:** Cron-job emails. If status is "Awaiting Payment" > 3 days, send a polite templated reminder.
*   **Lock-In:** Saves the freelancer emotional labor.

### 8. Calendar Feed (.ics Subscription)
*   **Function:** One-way sync to Google Calendar showing Project Due Dates and Payment Deadlines.
*   **Lock-In:** The app becomes part of their daily visual schedule.

### 9. Client "Rolodex" & LTV
*   **Function:** Address book showing Lifetime Value (LTV) per client. "You've earned $15k from Vertex Corp."
*   **Lock-In:** Revenue analytics are highly sticky.

### 10. "Rate Card" Hosting
*   **Function:** A public "Linktree-style" page listing services and prices. "Book Now" creates a draft project.
*   **Lock-In:** Becomes their primary sales brochure.

---

## 🔒 Phase 3: Assets & Security
*Tools that protect the work.*

### 11. Browser-Based Watermarking
*   **Function:** Client-side Canvas API burns a logo/text into images *before* upload.
*   **Value:** Theft deterrence without server processing costs.

### 12. File "Self-Destruct" Timer
*   **Function:** Option to auto-delete files from server X days after payment/download.
*   **Lock-In:** Creates urgency for the client; reduces storage costs for you.

### 13. Image "Pin" Feedback
*   **Function:** Click on image preview -> Drop Pin (x,y) -> Add Comment.
*   **Value:** Replaces "Frame.io" for static assets.

### 14. Contract Template Library (Mad Libs)
*   **Function:** "Fill-in-the-blank" text templates (NDA, SOW). Generates a PDF.
*   **Liability:** Zero (Disclaimer: "Drafting tool, not legal advice").
*   **Lock-In:** Convenience.

### 15. The "Audit Trail" PDF
*   **Function:** A downloadable receipt listing IP addresses, timestamps, and file hashes of access/downloads.
*   **Value:** Essential evidence for dispute resolution.

## 🚀 Phase 4: Growth & Marketing (The "Portfolio" Expansion)
*Features that turn the user's work into marketing material.*

### 16. The "Living" Portfolio (High Lock-In)
*   **Function:** A public profile (`truehandover.com/@username`). When a project is marked "Completed" & "Public", the thumbnail automatically appears on this grid.
*   **Lock-In:** If they leave TrueHandover, their professional website disappears.
*   **Cost:** Minimal (hosting thumbnails).

### 17. Client "Happiness" Rating (Private)
*   **Function:** After download, ask client: "How was working with [Freelancer]?" (1-5 stars).
*   **Value:** Private feedback loop for the freelancer (unlike public Upwork reviews).

### 18. "Verified Freelancer" Badge
*   **Function:** An embeddable HTML snippet for their *personal* website: "Payments Secured by TrueHandover."
*   **Value:** Adds legitimacy to them; free marketing for you.

### 19. QR Code Generator
*   **Function:** Generate QRs for specific vaults.
*   **Value:** Great for photographers/videographers doing in-person handovers.

### 20. Referral "Double Dip"
*   **Function:** "Invite a freelancer friend. You get 1GB storage, they get 1GB."
*   **Value:** Viral growth mechanism.

### 21. Moodboard Builder
*   **Function:** A pre-project grid where clients/freelancers dump inspiration images.
*   **Lock-In:** Captures the workflow *before* the contract even starts.

### 22. Retainer Subscription Links
*   **Function:** Generate a recurring Stripe payment link.
*   **Lock-In:** Extremely high. Moving platforms means asking clients to cancel/resubscribe.

## 🆕 Phase 5: Network & Intake
*Additional ideas requested for future expansion.*

### 23. Client Intake Form Builder
*   **Concept:** A simple form builder (Text, Date, Upload) for new leads. "Fill this out to start a project."
*   **Value:** Replaces Typeform/Google Forms. The data goes directly into a "Draft Project."
*   **Lock-In:** The freelancer embeds this on their site. Changing software breaks their sales funnel.

### 24. "Common Assets" Library
*   **Concept:** A shared folder for a specific client (e.g., "Logos", "Brand Fonts") that is accessible across *all* projects for that client.
*   **Value:** Reduces repetitive uploading.

### 25. Collaborator "Observer" Seats
*   **Concept:** Allow the Client to invite *their* boss/team to "View Only".
*   **Value:** Spreads TrueHandover usage to entire organizations (viral loop).

### 26. "One-Click" Case Study Generator
*   **Concept:** Takes the Project Name, Description, and Thumbnails -> Formats them into a clean HTML "Case Study" page.
*   **Value:** effortless content marketing for the freelancer.

### 27. Color Palette Extractor (Algorithmic)
*   **Concept:** Upload an image -> Javascript extracts the 5 dominant hex codes -> Saves to Project settings.
*   **No AI:** Uses simple color quantization algorithms (e.g., node-vibrant).

### 28. "Deposit" Calculator
*   **Concept:** A simple utility that splits a total fee into milestones (e.g., "50/50", "30/30/40") and auto-creates those milestones in the project.

### 29. Asset Version Tree
*   **Concept:** Visualizing file history (v1 -> v2 -> v3) like a git tree, rather than a flat list.
*   **Value:** Helps clients understand the volume of work/iterations performed.

### 30. Meeting Scheduler (Calendly Lite)
*   **Concept:** "Book a Review Call" button inside the Vault. Syncs with Google Calendar availability.
*   **Lock-In:** Eliminates the need for a separate Calendly subscription.

### 31. Export to ZIP
*   **Concept:** Allow clients to download *all* unlocked files at once as a generated .zip archive.
*   **Value:** Convenience for the client (Quality of Life).

### 32. "White-Label" Login Screen (CNAME)
*   **Concept:** Allow agencies to map `clients.their-domain.com` to the login page.
*   **Lock-In:** Extreme. The software becomes invisible; the freelancer looks like a tech giant.

## 🛠️ Phase 6: Operational & Utility (The "OS" Features)
*Features unrelated to delivery, focusing on daily freelance operations.*

### 33. The "Credential Vault" (Password Sharing)
*   **Concept:** A secure, encrypted text block within a project to share credentials (WP login, FTP, Socials).
*   **Value:** Replaces insecure emails.
*   **Lock-In:** Freelancers and clients rely on this as the "Source of Truth" for logins.

### 34. "Rights Transfer" Certificate Generator
*   **Concept:** Auto-generates a PDF certificate upon payment: "Official Transfer of IP Rights."
*   **Value:** Gives the client closure and a tangible asset for their payment.
*   **Lock-In:** Hard to replicate manually; adds "official" weight to the platform.

### 35. "The Morgue" (Unused Asset Store)
*   **Concept:** A personal storage folder for "Rejected Concepts" or "Unused Drafts" tagged by industry.
*   **Value:** Designers hate deleting work. This gives them a place to keep it for potential resale/inspiration.
*   **Lock-In:** Data gravity.

### 36. Availability/Booking Toggle
*   **Concept:** A simple status on the freelancer's public profile: "Booked until [Date]" or "Open for Work."
*   **Value:** Reduces "Are you available?" emails.
*   **Lock-In:** Becomes their primary status indicator to the world.

### 37. Expense "Shoebox"
*   **Concept:** Upload receipts (stock photos, fonts) associated with a project.
*   **Value:** Keeps project-specific costs organized for tax time.
*   **Lock-In:** Tax data is extremely sticky.

## 📦 Phase 7: Universal File Support (Vertical Integration)
*Expanding beyond graphic design to capture specific industries.*

### 38. Audio Vault (Music Industry)
*   **Target:** Music producers, voice-over artists, sound engineers.
*   **Formats:** MP3, WAV, AIFF.
*   **Function:** Client can listen to tracks via a secure player.
*   **Security:** **Audio Watermarking**. Server-side mix of an overlay voice (e.g., "Preview") every 10-15 seconds. Prevents "system audio recording" theft.
*   **Value:** Opens the platform to the massive audio freelance market.

### 39. 3D Model Viewer (Game Dev / Architecture)
*   **Target:** 3D modelers, architects, game devs.
*   **Formats:** GLB, OBJ, FBX.
*   **Function:** Interactive orbit/zoom viewer (Three.js) in the browser.
*   **Security:** Only the render is shown; the actual geometry file download is locked.
*   **Value:** Replaces static screenshots with interactive "proof of work".

### 40. Font Protection (Typography)
*   **Target:** Type designers, branding specialists.
*   **Formats:** TTF, OTF, WOFF.
*   **Function:** Users type custom text to test the font.
*   **Security:** **Critical.** Do not load the font file via CSS `@font-face` (inspect element risk). Use server-side rendering to generate **images** of the text on the fly.

### 41. RAW Photography Processing
*   **Target:** Wedding & Event Photographers.
*   **Formats:** CR2, NEF, ARW.
*   **Function:** Auto-convert huge RAW files to watermarked, compressed JPG previews.
*   **Value:** Solves the specific pain point of photographers sharing unedited selection galleries without giving away the negatives.

### 42. Code/Web Projects (Restricted Policy)
*   **Policy:** **No live HTML/JS execution.**
*   **Reason:** If the browser renders it, the client already has the code on their machine. "Save Page As" allows theft.
*   **Alternative:** Enforce Video Walkthroughs (Loom style) or Full-Page Screenshots for code deliverables to maintain the security promise.

### 43. Office Productivity Suite
*   **Target:** Consultants, Copywriters, Strategists.
*   **Formats:** DOCX, PPTX.
*   **Function:** Browser-based preview of Word documents and PowerPoint slides.
*   **Security:** Server-side conversion to read-only PDF/Images. Disables text selection and copying.
*   **Value:** Essential for strategy decks, scripts, and legal drafts.

### 44. Data & Spreadsheets
*   **Target:** SEO Agencies, Financial Analysts, Marketers.
*   **Formats:** CSV, XLSX, XLS.
*   **Function:** Interactive grid view of data.
*   **Security:** **"Row Locking"**. Display the first 20-50 rows clearly to prove the data exists and is valid. Blur/Lock the remaining rows until payment.
*   **Value:** Perfect for selling lead lists, keyword research, or financial models.

## 💸 Phase 8: Monetization & Add-ons
*Increasing ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) without complicating the tier structure.*

### 45. Storage Expansion Packs
*   **Concept:** Allow users on the "Studio" tier ($11.99) to purchase extra native storage without forcing an upgrade to the "Business" tier ($40).
*   **Pricing:** **$2.99/mo** for an additional **500GB**.
*   **Implementation:** Offer as a "Boost Storage" checkbox at checkout or a toggle in Billing Settings.
*   **Value:** Captures heavy users (e.g., video editors) who need raw space but don't care about agency features like White Labeling or CNAMEs. Keeps the pricing page clean (3 columns) while maximizing revenue.
